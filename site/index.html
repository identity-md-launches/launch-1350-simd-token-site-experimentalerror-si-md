Procedure

How to buy in 3 steps

$OOPS trades on Solana. You need a Solana wallet and a little SOL for the swap and network fees.

01 Get a Solana wallet

Install a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare on your phone or as a browser extension. Write the recovery phrase on paper and never type it into a website, including this one.

02 Get some SOL

Buy SOL on an exchange and send it to your wallet address, or use the buy option inside the wallet. Keep a little extra SOL for network fees. Fees on Solana are tiny, but they are not zero.

03 Swap on the coin page

Open the $OOPS coin page on pump.fun, connect your wallet there, enter the amount of SOL and confirm the swap. Check that the contract address on that page matches the one below before confirming.
